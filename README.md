**Heavily inspired by games in the rogue-like dungeon-crawling genre**, Dragonborne is a modpack completely focused around the exploration and combat aspects of Minecraft, where you can conquer countless dungeons, improve your skills and gear and fight mythical beasts beyond your imagination. The world has no regard for you, as time passes, monsters steadily grow stronger and you must find new ways to defend yourself!

### Features
- Completely unique classes, 5 classes were made specifically for Dragonborne! Each have their own style.
- Hundreds of new structures, delve underground or explore ruins and castles above.
- Simple, open ended progression, you must defeat 12 out of 16 Legendary bosses to unlock access to the final boss.
- Build variety, these range from claymore-wielding berserkers to glass-cannon electromancers.
- Monster variety, there's dozens of monster variants that are significantly tougher than vanilla.
- Roguelike loot modifiers, no weapon, tome, charm or armor set is the same.
- Improved levelling system, you can spend your XP on stats and skills to survive easier.
- Progressively harder difficulty, mobs grow stronger and stronger over time.
- No annoying quests, everything is simple to understand if you read a little.
- Improvements to all non-combat areas, for when you want to take a break.
...and much, much more!

### Installation
Go to the [releases](https://github.com/Karmabound/spellbound-modpack/releases) tab, then unzip the files into a new installation of 1.20.1 Forge.
For servers, simply unzip the dragonborne-x.x-server files into a folder, then click run.bat! Works perfectly with playit.gg!

### Performance
- It is recommended to allocate atleast 5GB of RAM to your instance, but don't go further than 8.
- Servers should have around 6GB RAM allocated, otherwise they might lag behind.
